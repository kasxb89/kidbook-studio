## 温故知新 旧书里的新发现

依据成语常用含义创作的原创古代生活故事，并非该成语的历史典故。

创作说明：阿祁、先生与整理竹筹的情节为虚构。通过重看旧记录、重做配对数物练习，发现摆放间距与数量不同，表现复习带来的新理解。

成语含义：复习已经学过的知识，从中获得新的理解。

Review what you have learned and gain a fresh understanding.

寓意：重做熟悉的练习时，也可以问问为什么，发现从前没想明白的地方。

When you revisit a familiar lesson, ask why and notice something you did not understand before.

日常用法：把旧课重看一遍，又有了新发现，这就是温故知新。

Reviewing an old lesson and discovering something new is learning afresh from what we already know.

### 第 1 页

阿祁在书架下找出一本旧小记，封面的布角已经软软的。

A Qi found an old notebook beneath the bookshelf. Its cloth cover was soft at the corners.

### 第 2 页

“这是我从前学数东西时用的。”他抱着小记坐到先生身边。

“I used this when I first learned to count things,” he said, sitting beside his teacher.

### 第 3 页

书里画着小木块和竹筹，阿祁一下子想起了以前的练习。

Sketches of wooden blocks and bamboo rods reminded A Qi of an earlier lesson.

### 第 4 页

先生拿来四块小木块，又把四根竹筹轻轻放在桌上。

The teacher brought four wooden blocks and laid four bamboo rods on the table.

### 第 5 页

阿祁把一根筹放在一块木块旁边，很快就排好了四对。

A Qi placed one rod beside each block. Soon there were four matching pairs.

### 第 6 页

“这个我会！”阿祁笑了。先生问：“为什么一块配一根呢？”

“I know this!” A Qi smiled. “Why match one rod with one block?” asked the teacher.

### 第 7 页

阿祁想了想：“这样，每一块都能点到，也不会点两次。”

A Qi thought. “Then I can count every block without counting one twice.”

### 第 8 页

先生没有添木块，只把它们之间的空隙拉大了一点。

Without adding any blocks, the teacher spread them a little farther apart.

### 第 9 页

阿祁望着长长的一排，小声说：“看起来，好像多了些。”

A Qi looked at the longer row. “It looks as though there are more.”

### 第 10 页

“先别急着说多了。”先生请他再用竹筹配一配。

“Let's check before deciding,” said the teacher. “Match the rods with them again.”

### 第 11 页

阿祁一块一块地配。四根竹筹刚好用完，没有多，也没有少。

A Qi matched them one by one. All four rods fitted, with none extra or missing.

### 第 12 页

他又把木块拢近些，点了一遍，还是原来的四块。

He moved the blocks closer and counted again. There were still the same four.

### 第 13 页

“原来，摆得长一些，不等于东西变多了。”阿祁抬起头。

“So a longer row does not mean there are more things!” A Qi looked up.

### 第 14 页

先生笑着点头：“从前学的办法，还能帮你想明白新的问题。”

The teacher nodded. “A lesson you already know can help you understand a new question.”

### 第 15 页

阿祁重新翻开旧小记。这回，他想的不只是怎样排好竹筹。

A Qi opened the old notebook again. This time, he thought beyond arranging the rods.

### 第 16 页

他还想看看：这些办法为什么有用，换个样子又会怎样。

He wanted to know why the method worked and what happened when things were arranged differently.

### 第 17 页

午后的光落在旧书上。阿祁觉得，熟悉的一页也有新的发现。

Afternoon light fell across the old book. A familiar page had brought him a fresh discovery.

### 第 18 页

温故知新，是重温已经学过的知识，从中获得新的理解。

Reviewing what we have learned can bring us a new understanding.


## 不耻下问 年纪小，也能教我

依据成语常用含义创作的原创古代生活故事，并非该成语的历史典故。

创作说明：文青、小蓁和先生为虚构人物。采用向年纪更小的熟悉同伴请教折纸的情境；“不耻”指愿意谦虚请教，不对不会的孩子作羞辱或能力排名。

成语含义：向比自己年纪小、地位低或学识不如自己的人请教，不觉得可耻。

Be willing to learn from someone younger, of lower status, or with less knowledge than yourself, without feeling ashamed.

寓意：每个人都有会做的事，向年纪小的人认真请教，也能学到新办法。

Everyone knows something. Asking someone younger can help us learn a new way.

日常用法：弟弟会的折法我还不会，我向他请教，也是不耻下问。

My younger brother knows a fold I do not. I can learn by asking him.

### 第 1 页

文青坐在院里的小桌旁，想折一只底边平平的纸舟。

Wen Qing sat at the little courtyard table, hoping to fold a paper boat with a flat base.

### 第 2 页

纸角绕来绕去，他刚把一边压好，另一边又松开了。

He folded and pressed the corners, but one side loosened whenever he fixed the other.

### 第 3 页

文青再试一次，纸舟还是歪歪的，摆在桌上站不稳。

He tried again. The crooked boat still would not sit steadily on the table.

### 第 4 页

小蓁从廊下走来，把自己折好的小纸舟放到桌边。

Xiao Zhen came from the veranda and placed her finished little boat beside him.

### 第 5 页

她的纸舟底边平平，轻轻放下，就稳稳地停在那里。

Her boat had a flat base. It sat steadily when she set it down.

### 第 6 页

文青看了看她，又看了看自己的纸角，想问，却没开口。

Wen Qing looked at her, then at his loose corner. He wanted to ask but stayed quiet.

### 第 7 页

“她比我小……”文青心里嘀咕，“向她问，会不会不好意思？”

“She's younger than I am,” he thought. “Would it feel embarrassing to ask her?”

### 第 8 页

先生走到桌旁，笑着说：“年纪小的人，也会有你还不会的事。”

The teacher joined them. “Someone younger may know something you have not learned yet.”

### 第 9 页

文青点点头，转向小蓁：“你能教我，怎样把这个角折稳吗？”

Wen Qing nodded. “Could you show me how to make this corner stay in place?”

### 第 10 页

“可以呀。”小蓁把自己的纸舟轻轻展开，指给他看先折哪边。

“Of course.” Xiao Zhen gently opened her boat to show him which side to fold first.

### 第 11 页

文青认真看着，一步没有看清，便请她再慢慢做一遍。

Wen Qing watched carefully. When he missed a step, he asked her to show it slowly again.

### 第 12 页

小蓁先对齐两条边，再压住小角。文青照着她的办法试了试。

Xiao Zhen aligned two edges and pressed the little corner. Wen Qing tried it the same way.

### 第 13 页

这一次，小角没有滑开。文青轻轻压平底边，又检查了一遍。

This time the corner stayed put. Wen Qing flattened the base and checked it once more.

### 第 14 页

他把纸舟放在桌上，慢慢松开手。小纸舟终于站稳了。

He placed the boat on the table and slowly let go. At last it sat steadily.

### 第 15 页

“谢谢你！”文青笑了，“刚才不好意思问，差点错过这个办法。”

“Thank you!” Wen Qing smiled. “I almost missed your helpful idea because I was shy about asking.”

### 第 16 页

小蓁也笑了：“下次我有不会的，也想来问问你。”

Xiao Zhen smiled too. “When I need help, I'd like to ask you as well.”

### 第 17 页

两只纸舟并排停着。文青觉得，认真请教，比闷着头发愁好多了。

The boats sat side by side. Asking carefully felt much better than worrying alone.

### 第 18 页

不耻下问，是向比自己年幼或地位、学识不如自己的人请教，也不觉得可耻。

We need not feel ashamed to learn from someone younger, of lower status, or with less knowledge than ourselves.


## 集思广益 读书亭怎样更舒服

依据成语常用含义创作的原创古代生活故事，并非该成语的历史典故。

创作说明：若宁、伙伴、祖母与村口读书亭为虚构。讨论着重收集、比较和综合不同需要；安装遮阳布与稳固木凳由木匠完成，孩子只参与轻便布置。

成语含义：集中大家的想法，让办法更完善。

Bring together different people's ideas to make a better plan.

寓意：先听听大家各自发现了什么，再把适合的想法放在一起，办法会更周全。

Listen to what others have noticed and combine the ideas that fit to make a more thoughtful plan.

日常用法：怎样布置共读角？大家集思广益，一起想个周全的办法。

How should we arrange our reading corner? Let's gather ideas and make a thoughtful plan.

### 第 1 页

村口有座小亭子，若宁和伙伴想在这里放几本书，让人坐下读一读。

There was a little pavilion at the village entrance. Ruo Ning and her friend hoped to make it a reading spot.

### 第 2 页

午后的阳光斜斜照进来。若宁刚坐下，就抬手挡了挡眼睛。

Afternoon sunlight slanted inside. Ruo Ning raised a hand to shade her eyes as she sat down.

### 第 3 页

“挂一块布，把这一边遮住吧。”她想，遮住太阳就舒服了。

“Let's hang some cloth on this side,” she suggested. Shade would make reading more comfortable.

### 第 4 页

伙伴站到另一边，发现风正从亭子两侧轻轻穿过去。

Her friend stood on the other side and felt a breeze passing through the pavilion.

### 第 5 页

“遮住这一边，风还能从哪里进来呢？”他把自己的想法说出来。

“If we shade this side, where will the breeze come in?” he asked.

### 第 6 页

若宁想了想：“原来，我只顾着挡太阳，还没想到风怎么进来呢。”

Ruo Ning thought. “I was thinking about the sun. I hadn’t considered where the breeze would come in.”

### 第 7 页

祖母来歇脚，慢慢坐下：“凳子也要稳，脚下的路得留出来。”

Grandmother stopped to rest. “The bench must be steady, and the path should stay clear.”

### 第 8 页

她指指亭口。来往的人经过这里，需要有地方慢慢走过去。

She pointed to the entrance. People passing by needed enough room to walk through.

### 第 9 页

若宁没有马上决定。她请大家说说，各自最在意的是什么。

Ruo Ning did not decide at once. She asked what mattered most to each of them.

### 第 10 页

他们把几条想法放在一起：遮阳、通风、坐稳，还要留出通路。

They brought their ideas together: shade, a breeze, steady seating, and a clear path.

### 第 11 页

木匠听完，走进亭子看了看：“只遮最晒的那一侧，怎么样？”

A carpenter listened and looked around. “What if we shade only the sunniest side?”

### 第 12 页

“另一侧留着风口，凳子靠里面，亭口也不放东西。”大家点点头。

“Leave the other side open, move the bench inward, and keep the entrance clear.” Everyone nodded.

### 第 13 页

木匠把遮阳布装稳，又检查木凳。孩子们在旁边等他做完。

The carpenter secured the shade cloth and checked the bench. The children waited until he finished.

### 第 14 页

若宁和伙伴只搬来轻轻的书篮，把书摆到伸手就能拿到的地方。

Ruo Ning and her friend brought a light book basket and put it within easy reach.

### 第 15 页

太阳再斜过来时，读书的地方有了影子，风也还轻轻吹着。

When the sun slanted in again, the reading spot was shaded, and a breeze still passed through.

### 第 16 页

祖母坐得稳稳的。亭口留着宽宽的路，走过去也很方便。

Grandmother sat comfortably on the steady bench. The wide entrance remained easy to pass through.

### 第 17 页

若宁笑了：“每个人多想一点，我们的办法就周全一些。”

Ruo Ning smiled. “Each person's thought helped us make a more complete plan.”

### 第 18 页

集思广益，是把大家的想法汇集起来，让办法更完善。

Bringing different people's ideas together can make a plan better.


## 兼听则明 纸灯到底去了哪里

依据成语常用含义创作的原创古代生活故事，并非该成语的历史典故。

创作说明：以雨后寻找借来纸灯的原创情节说明兼听则明。两位孩子描述不同时间发生的移动，先生帮助听完并核对先后，再到廊下查看；不同说法不自动等于谁在说谎。纸灯始终未点亮。

成语含义：多听不同方面的意见与经过，才更容易了解事情。

Listening to different accounts and views can help us understand a situation more clearly.

寓意：先听完不同的经过，再核对时间和现场，事情才容易看清。

Listen to different accounts, then check the timing and what is there to understand what happened.

日常用法：两位同学说的经过不一样，先分别听完再核对，兼听则明。

Two classmates tell different parts of a story. Listening to both and checking what happened helps us understand it.

### 第 1 页

雨停了，景和想把借来的小纸灯收好，却发现灯架空空的。

After the rain, Jing He went to put away the borrowed paper lamp. Its rack was empty.

### 第 2 页

他向窗边的小桌走去：“我记得，纸灯放在这里。”

He went to the table by the window. “I remember the lamp was here.”

### 第 3 页

小悠听见了，说：“我看到它在廊下，不在窗边呀。”

Xiao You heard him. “I saw it on the veranda, not by the window.”

### 第 4 页

景和皱起眉：“可我明明看见了。”两个人都有些疑惑。

“But I saw it there,” Jing He said, frowning. Both children were puzzled.

### 第 5 页

先生请他们坐下：“先把经过说完，看看是不是同一个时候。”

The teacher invited them to sit. “Tell me what happened. Were you looking at the same time?”

### 第 6 页

景和先说：“刚下雨时，灯还在檐边，纸角被雨飘湿了。”

Jing He began. “When the rain started, the lamp was near the eaves. Rain dampened one edge.”

### 第 7 页

“我把它拿进来，放到窗边小桌上，然后去收院里的书篮。”

“I brought it inside and put it on the window table. Then I went to collect the book basket.”

### 第 8 页

先生点点头，又转向小悠：“后来，你看到了什么？”

The teacher nodded and turned to Xiao You. “What did you see later?”

### 第 9 页

小悠说：“等雨停了，我来到窗边，纸灯的边缘还潮潮的。”

“After the rain stopped, I came to the window,” said Xiao You. “The lamp's edge was still damp.”

### 第 10 页

“我问过先生，就把它移到有屋檐遮着的廊下，让风吹一吹。”

“I asked our teacher, then moved it to the sheltered veranda so the breeze could dry it.”

### 第 11 页

景和听完，轻轻点头：“原来，你说的是后来的事。”

Jing He nodded. “So you were telling me what happened afterward.”

### 第 12 页

小悠也明白了：“你说的窗边，是我移灯以前的地方。”

Xiao You understood too. “The window table was where it was before I moved it.”

### 第 13 页

先生把先后串起来：“先从灯架到窗边，再从窗边到廊下。”

The teacher put the events in order. “First, from the rack to the table. Then, to the veranda.”

### 第 14 页

“我们再去看看吧。”三个人沿着平平的廊子走过去。

“Let's go and check.” The three of them walked along the level veranda.

### 第 15 页

小纸灯果然挂在那里，靛蓝的小流苏被风轻轻吹动。

There was the lamp. Its little indigo tassel swayed gently in the breeze.

### 第 16 页

先生摸摸纸边，等它干了，再和孩子们一起把灯收好。

The teacher checked the paper edge. Once it was dry, they put the lamp away together.

### 第 17 页

景和笑了：“只听一段，我就只知道纸灯走过的一段路。”

Jing He smiled. “Hearing only one part tells me only part of the lamp's journey.”

### 第 18 页

兼听则明，是多听不同方面的意见和经过，再核对，才能看得更清楚。

Listening to different views and accounts, then checking the facts, helps us understand more clearly.


## 实事求是 水缸真的漏了吗

依据成语常用含义创作的原创古代生活故事，并非该成语的历史典故。

创作说明：以茶坊水缸水量变化的原创故事说明依据实际情况判断。先检查缸身与地面，再在无人取水的一小段时间内核对水位，并查看取水和添水经过；结论限于当天观察，不把干地面当作绝不漏水的证明。

成语含义：从实际情况出发，依据查清的事实判断和处理事情。

Start with the actual situation and use checked facts to make judgments and decide what to do.

寓意：猜测可以提醒我们去查一查，判断却要有查清的事实作依据。

A guess can give us something to check, but our judgment should be based on facts we have checked.

日常用法：种子没发芽，先看看土和水的实际情况，再找原因，这就是实事求是。

When seeds have not sprouted, check the soil and water before deciding why.

### 第 1 页

茶坊刚忙过一阵，祖父揭开水缸盖，发现水比早上少了许多。

After a busy spell at the tea shop, Grandfather lifted the jar lid. Much less water remained than that morning.

### 第 2 页

阿衡睁大眼：“水少得这么快，会不会是缸漏了？”

A Heng's eyes widened. “Could the jar be leaking? The water has gone down so fast!”

### 第 3 页

祖父没有马上点头：“这是个猜想，我们看看实际情况。”

Grandfather did not agree at once. “That's a guess. Let's check what is happening.”

### 第 4 页

他们绕着水缸看了一圈。缸身没有看见裂口，地面也没有水迹。

They looked around the jar. They saw no crack in its sides and no wet patch on the ground.

### 第 5 页

阿衡刚想说“不漏”，祖父提醒：“这些还不能让我们下结论。”

A Heng was about to say it wasn't leaking. Grandfather said, “We need more than that to decide.”

### 第 6 页

祖父用一小团湿泥，在缸外与当前水面同高的地方做了记号。

Grandfather placed a little damp clay outside the jar, level with the water inside.

### 第 7 页

接下来的一小段时间，他们不取水，也不添水，等着看水位。

For a little while, they took no water out and added none. They waited to check the level.

### 第 8 页

两人坐在院边，翻开今天的用水小记，先看早上添了多少水。

They sat nearby with the day's water-use notes and checked how much water had been added that morning.

### 第 9 页

“今天开门早，我还没添满，就有客人来了。”祖父想起来了。

“We opened early today,” Grandfather remembered. “Customers arrived before I had filled the jar.”

### 第 10 页

阿衡又指着小记：“这里多画了一壶，今天多泡了一壶茶。”

A Heng pointed to another sketch. “There's an extra teapot here. You made an extra pot of tea today.”

### 第 11 页

祖父还想起，开门后他取过水，冲洗茶壶，也擦过几张茶桌。

Grandfather also remembered using water to rinse the teapot and wipe several tea tables after opening.

### 第 12 页

过了一会儿，他们再看水缸，水面仍和刚才的记号一样高。

After a while, they checked the jar again. The water was still level with the mark.

### 第 13 页

祖父说：“刚才没有取水时，水位没变。今天还少添了、多用了。”

Grandfather said, “The level stayed steady while we left it alone. Today we also added less and used more.”

### 第 14 页

Yu An looked up. “I wanted to read with you, but I didn't know why you had put it away.”

### 第 9 页

阿澄又说：“你刚洗完手，我还担心手上的水滴会留在纸上。”

A Cheng added, “You had just washed your hands. I was also worried about water getting on the pages.”

### 第 10 页

“那我先把手擦干。”语安说，“下次收在哪里，也告诉我，好吗？”

“I'll dry my hands first,” said Yu An. “Could you also tell me where you put it next time?”

### 第 11 页

阿澄点头：“好。它是我们一起看的，我不是想一个人藏着。”

A Cheng nodded. “Yes. It's for us both. I wasn't trying to keep it to myself.”

### 第 12 页

听完彼此的话，两人的眉头都松开了。原来，担心和需要可以说清楚。

After listening to each other, both relaxed. They could explain their worries and what they needed.

### 第 13 页

祖母问：“怎样既能一起看，又能照顾好画册？”他们指向屋里的干桌子。

“How can you read together and care for the album?” Grandmother asked. They pointed to the dry table inside.

### 第 14 页

他们约好：擦干手，在干桌上一起看；读完收回木箱，挪了位置就告诉对方。

They agreed to dry their hands, read at the dry table, return the album afterward, and tell each other if they moved it.

### 第 15 页

两人先把手擦干。阿澄打开箱子，拿出那本熟悉的画册。

They dried their hands. A Cheng opened the chest and took out the familiar album.

### 第 16 页

画册摊在桌上，语安指着一幅花园图。阿澄挨着她，笑着看下去。

The album lay open on the table. Yu An pointed to a garden picture, and A Cheng read beside her, smiling.

### 第 17 页

读完，他们一起收好画册。阿澄说：“把话说清，我们就更容易一起用了。”

They put the album away together. “Explaining things makes it easier to share,” said A Cheng.

### 第 18 页

开诚布公，是带着诚意，把共同事情里的想法和顾虑坦白地说清。

Speak sincerely and openly about the thoughts and concerns that matter to something you share.

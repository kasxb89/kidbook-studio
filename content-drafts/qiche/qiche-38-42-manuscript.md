## 红色跑车，怎么坐上了大卡车？

去另一座城市参展的出发发现

### 第 1 页

车展结束后的另一个周末上午，安安抱着小熊，和妈妈散步经过银顶展馆外的小广场。她忽然停住脚步：“妈妈，是那辆红色跑车！”

**English**

On another weekend morning after the car show, An'an held Bear as she and Mom walked past the plaza outside the silver-roofed exhibition hall. She suddenly stopped. “Mom, it's the red sports car!”

### 第 2 页

这回，红色跑车没有在道路上奔跑，也没停在展台旁，而是安静地待在一辆长长的大卡车背上。安安睁大了眼睛。

**English**

This time, the red sports car was neither driving on the road nor resting beside a display stage. Instead, it sat quietly on the back of a long truck. An'an's eyes grew wide.

### 第 3 页

穿白上衣、海蓝色长裤的张姐姐从参观区旁走过来，微笑着向安安挥挥手。安安连忙问：“红色跑车坏了吗？它怎么自己不开走呀？”

**English**

Wearing her white polo shirt and sea-blue trousers, Zhang walked over beside the viewing area and waved with a smile. An'an asked right away, “Is the red sports car broken? Why isn't it driving by itself?”

### 第 4 页

张姐姐笑着摇头：“它没有坏，也能在公路上正常开。只是这次，它要和几辆展车一起去另一座很远的城市参加车展。”

**English**

Zhang smiled and shook her head. “It isn't broken, and it can drive on roads too. This time, though, it's traveling with several other show cars to an exhibition in a distant city.”

### 第 5 页

“那辆长长的大卡车，叫汽车运输车。”张姐姐指着远处的车架说，“它一次能带好几辆汽车出门，就像汽车们一起坐大巴去远方。”

**English**

“That long truck is called a car carrier,” Zhang said, pointing toward the trailer frame. “It can take several cars on one trip, almost like cars riding a coach together to a faraway place.”

### 第 6 页

安安数了数运输车上的汽车：“一、二、三、四、五！原来一辆运输车出发，这些展车就不用各自跑那么远的路啦。”

**English**

An'an counted the cars on the carrier. “One, two, three, four, five! When one carrier sets off, all these show cars don't each have to drive that long distance.”

### 第 7 页

安安歪着头看向上下两层：“汽车又不会蹦跳，它们是怎么爬到运输车上面去的呢？”张姐姐请她来到旁边的讲解桌前。

**English**

An'an tilted her head toward the two levels. “Cars can't jump—how did they climb onto the carrier?” Zhang invited her over to a nearby demonstration table.

### 第 8 页

讲解桌上摆着一辆运输车教学模型。张姐姐轻轻放下模型车尾的斜板：“你看，尾板放下来，就变成了一条平缓的坡道。”

**English**

A teaching model of a car carrier sat on the table. Zhang gently lowered the hinged ramp at the back. “Look—when the tail ramp comes down, it becomes a gentle slope.”

### 第 9 页

张姐姐推着小模型车，顺着坡道慢慢开上第一层平台，再把上层平台稳稳托起、锁好。安安说：“上层像坐电梯一样升起来了！”

**English**

Zhang rolled a miniature car slowly up the ramp onto the deck, then raised and locked the upper platform in place. “The upper deck went up like a little elevator!” said An'an.

### 第 10 页

安安看看模型的两层车架：“上面一层，下面一层，每辆车都有自己的位置，头顶和前后还留着空隙，不会互相碰着。”

**English**

An'an looked at the two levels of the model frame. “One level above and one below—each car has its own spot, with space overhead and between them so they don't bump.”

### 第 11 页

“光停上去，可以出发了吗？”张姐姐先把模型上的小车直接摆在平台中央，没有扣上固定块，再把模型底座轻轻抬高一点点。

**English**

“Once they're parked on the deck, are they ready to go?” Zhang left a miniature car loose on the model platform without wheel stops, then tilted the model base just a little.

### 第 12 页

骨碌碌！小模型车顺着平台往后滑，被张姐姐稳稳接住。安安倒吸一口气：“哇，路上要是遇到上下坡或者刹车，它会滑动呀！”

**English**

Roll, roll! The tiny model car slid backward along the deck until Zhang caught it safely. An'an gasped. “Oh! On a real hill or when braking, an unsecured car could slide!”

### 第 13 页

张姐姐拿出小小的止退块和车轮固定带模型，把小模型车的四个轮子稳稳固定在平台孔位上，再轻轻倾斜底座——这回，小车纹丝不动。

**English**

Zhang placed miniature wheel chocks and wheel straps over all four wheels of the model car, anchoring them to the deck slots, then tilted the base again. This time, the little car did not budge.

### 第 14 页

回到隔离栏旁，安安看向真正的汽车运输车。两位穿反光背心的工作人员正在做最后检查，每辆展车的车轮都扣着结实的固定带。

**English**

Back beside the safety barrier, An'an looked at the full-size car carrier. Two crew members in high-visibility vests were doing a final check, with sturdy straps around every show car's wheels.

### 第 15 页

工作人员确认所有车辆都固定妥当，操作收起后方的坡道尾板，再锁好安全栓。长长的运输车做好了出发准备。

**English**

After confirming that every car was secured, the crew raised the rear loading ramp and locked the safety latches. The long car carrier was ready for departure.

### 第 16 页

蓝色牵引车头平稳启动，带着一车展车慢慢驶出广场。安安向红色跑车挥挥手：“上次你在展台上，这次你也成了稳稳的小乘客！”

**English**

The blue tractor cab started smoothly, pulling its load of show cars out of the plaza. An'an waved to the red sports car. “Last time you were on the stage—today you're a steady passenger!”

### 第 17 页

中午回到家，安安把积木倒在客厅地毯上，给玩具大卡车搭起一层长长的平板，又把玩具小跑车和小轿车摆了上去。

**English**

Back home at midday, An'an poured her blocks onto the living-room rug. She built a long flat platform on a toy truck and placed her toy sports car and toy sedan on top.

### 第 18 页

安安刚推着积木卡车经过地毯边的小斜坡，上面的玩具跑车就往后滑了下来。“哎呀，我忘了给车轮加挡块啦！”她恍然大悟。

**English**

As soon as An'an pushed her block truck over a little ramp on the rug, the toy sports car slid off the back. “Oh! I forgot to put stops by the wheels!” she realized.

### 第 19 页

她在每辆玩具车的前后车轮旁，都嵌进两块长条小积木当止退块。再推着积木大卡车上坡、转弯，上面的玩具车都稳稳当当！

**English**

She fitted two small block strips in front of and behind each toy car's wheels as chocks. When she pushed the block truck up the slope and around a turn again, the toy cars stayed right in place!

### 第 20 页

下午，妈妈带安安去山那边的汽车养护中心给家里的小汽车做检查。刚坐进后排儿童安全座椅驶上街道，前车闪动的小灯又让她好奇起来。

**English**

That afternoon, Mom took An'an to the car care center beyond the mountain for their family car's checkup. Soon after An'an buckled into her rear child seat and they drove onto the street, a blinking light on the car ahead sparked her next question.

### 亲子共读

共读目标：认识汽车运输车的双层平台、尾板坡道与车轮固定装置，理解为什么会自己开的汽车在跨城参展或集中调运时也会“坐大卡车”。

家长提醒：不要把跑车说成不能在普通道路行驶的车辆；集中运输是为了长途调运或多车同行。真实装卸区属于专业作业范围，孩子只在隔离区外观察，通过桌面模型或家中积木体验斜坡与止退固定。

可以聊一聊：

- 红色跑车明明自己会开，为什么这次要坐上汽车运输车去另一座城市？
- 如果没有止退块和固定带，运输车在上下坡或刹车时，平台上的车会发生什么？
- 你在家里给积木卡车装玩具车时，想用什么办法帮它们稳稳停住？

桌面小游戏：在地毯或桌面上用硬纸板和积木搭一辆带斜坡尾板的“汽车运输车”，先把玩具小车直接放在光滑纸板上轻轻倾斜，观察滑动；再用小积木条或防滑垫挡在玩具车轮前后，对比固定前后的变化。全程在室内地面或桌面缓慢操作，不攀爬家具、不计时。

4–5 岁：一起找一找运输车的上层、下层、尾板坡道和固定带，用玩具车体验“顺坡上去、挡住车轮、稳稳出发”。

6–8 岁：讨论一车多运在长途参展中的好处，并尝试在积木平台上同时安排两辆不同高矮的玩具车，思考怎样留出高度和前后间距。

## 小汽车的灯，会说话吗？

去汽车养护中心路上的小信号

### 第 1 页

星期六下午，妈妈开着浅蓝色小汽车，带安安去山那边的汽车养护中心。安安系好后排儿童安全座椅，小熊安静地坐在她腿上。

**English**

On Saturday afternoon, Mom drove their light-blue car toward the car care center beyond the mountain. An'an sat buckled in her rear child seat, with Bear resting quietly in her lap.

### 第 2 页

前面那辆银灰色小汽车的左后角，忽然有一盏小黄灯一闪、一闪。安安睁大眼睛问：“妈妈，它是在朝我们眨眼睛吗？”

**English**

At the left rear corner of the silver-gray car ahead, a small amber light began blinking on and off. An'an widened her eyes. “Mom, is that car winking at us?”

### 第 3 页

妈妈笑着说：“那是左转向灯。它不会真的说话，却能替驾驶人告诉周围：我准备往左边转弯，或者往左边换车道啦。”

**English**

Mom smiled. “That's the left turn signal. It can't speak with words, but it lets everyone nearby know that the driver plans to turn left or move into the left lane.”

### 第 4 页

银灰色小汽车先闪了几下左转向灯，看清左边车道有足够空间，才平平稳稳地并过去。安安点点头：“先提醒，再动作！”

**English**

After blinking its left signal several times and making sure the left lane was clear, the silver-gray car glided smoothly over. An'an nodded. “Remind others first, then move!”

### 第 5 页

不一会儿，右边车道的蓝绿色公交车亮起了右边的小黄灯，一闪一闪地靠近站台。安安马上认出来了：“它准备向右靠站！”

**English**

Soon after, a teal-blue city bus in the right lane blinked its right amber signal as it approached a bus stop. An'an recognized it right away. “It's getting ready to pull over to the right!”

### 第 6 页

继续往前走，正前方一辆深蓝色小汽车的车尾，忽然同时亮起两盏红红的灯，不闪也不灭。安安问：“这又是哪句话呀？”

**English**

Farther along, two bright red lights suddenly came on together at the back of a dark-blue car ahead, shining steadily without blinking. “What is it saying now?” asked An'an.

### 第 7 页

妈妈也轻轻踩下刹车，和前车保持距离：“这是红色的刹车灯。它亮起来，是在提醒后面的车：我正在减速，或者准备停下。”

**English**

Mom gently pressed the brake pedal too, keeping a safe distance behind the car ahead. “Those are red brake lights. When they light up, they tell drivers behind: I'm slowing down or getting ready to stop.”

### 第 8 页

到了前面的路口，一辆白色小汽车停在右转车道最前面，右边的小黄灯一闪一闪。安安说：“它亮右转向灯了，要右转啦！”

**English**

At the next intersection, a white car waited at the front of the right-turn lane with its right amber signal blinking. “Its right turn signal is on!” said An'an. “It wants to turn right!”

### 第 9 页

可是，白色小汽车的小黄灯闪了好几下，轮子却一动也没动。安安有些奇怪：“它已经告诉大家要右转了，为什么还不走呢？”

**English**

Yet even after its amber light blinked several times, the white car's wheels did not move at all. An'an wondered, “It already told everyone it plans to turn right—why isn't it going?”

### 第 10 页

妈妈请安安看向右侧的斑马线。一位阿姨正牵着小朋友按绿色行人灯过马路，旁边的非机动车道上，还有一位叔叔骑着自行车直行。

**English**

Mom asked An'an to look at the crosswalk on the right. A woman and a child were crossing with their green pedestrian signal, and a man was riding a bicycle straight through in the bike lane beside them.

### 第 11 页

妈妈温和地说：“转向灯只是说出自己的打算，不是让大家都给自己让路。转弯前，还是要看清周围，先礼让正在通行的人。”

**English**

“A turn signal only shares a driver's plan,” Mom explained gently. “It doesn't mean everyone else must get out of the way. Before turning, a driver still checks around and yields to people who have the right to go first.”

### 第 12 页

等行人和骑车人都安全过去了，白色小汽车才慢慢右转。安安恍然大悟：“原来，我们看别人的车灯，也要看它是不是真的停稳、让行！”

**English**

Only after the pedestrians and cyclist were safely across did the white car turn right slowly. An'an understood. “So even when we see a car's lights, we still watch what the car is actually doing!”

### 第 13 页

穿过路口后，前方出现了通往山脚道路的指示牌。妈妈准备向右变换车道，她先拨动转向灯杆，仪表盘上的绿色小箭头跟着轻轻闪烁。

**English**

Past the intersection, a direction sign pointed toward the mountain road ahead. Preparing to move into the right lane, Mom flicked the signal stalk first, and a small green arrow blinked on the dashboard.

### 第 14 页

妈妈先看后视镜，确认右侧车道有足够的安全距离，才带着小汽车平稳并入右侧车道。拨杆归位，一闪一闪的转向灯也停了下来。

**English**

Mom checked her mirrors first and made sure there was plenty of safe space in the right lane before guiding the car smoothly across. As the lane change finished, the blinking turn signal clicked off.

### 第 15 页

安安低头对怀里的小熊说：“黄灯闪一闪是提醒方向，红灯亮起来是提醒减速。回家后，我也要给玩具车贴上小灯图案，请你猜猜它想做什么！”

**English**

An'an looked down at Bear in her lap. “A blinking amber light shares a direction, and bright red lights show slowing down. When we get home, I'll put little light stickers on my toy car so you can guess what it plans to do!”

### 第 16 页

正说着，一座高高的青山出现在前方，山脚下有一道半圆形的大拱门。安安望着前面的山：“妈妈，大山挡在前面，小汽车要钻进山里去吗？”

**English**

Just then, a tall green mountain rose ahead, with a wide arched portal at its foot. An'an looked out at the mountain. “Mom, the mountain is right in front of us—is our car going to go inside it?”

### 亲子共读

共读目标：认识汽车的左/右转向灯与红色刹车灯，理解车灯是传递行车意图的信号；同时懂得打灯不等于拥有优先权，通行仍需观察周围与礼让。

家长提醒：引导孩子区分“表达打算”与“实际行动”：驾驶人打转向灯后仍须观察后视镜、礼让行人和直行车辆；行人在路上看到车辆打灯或不打灯，都不能只凭灯光猜测车辆一定停下或一定不转弯，应在成人陪同下确认安全。

可以聊一聊：

- 看到前车左边或右边的小黄灯一闪一闪，或者车尾两盏红灯一起亮起，分别表示什么？
- 为什么那辆白色小汽车已经亮了右转向灯，却还在路口停着等一等？
- 如果你给玩具车贴上黄色和红色的小纸片，你想怎样用它们跟小熊做提醒游戏？

桌面小游戏：在家里准备几张黄色和红色的小圆点贴纸，贴在玩具汽车的前后左右角和车尾。大人或孩子在桌面纸路推动玩具车前，先指一指要亮哪盏“小灯”，再检查纸路上的玩偶行人和其它玩具车，练习“先打灯提醒、再观察让行、最后平稳移动”。全程在桌面进行，不设倒计时。

4–5 岁：分辨左边闪、右边闪和车尾两盏红灯同时亮，用玩具车做“闪一闪、看一看、让一让、慢慢走”的动作游戏。

6–8 岁：讨论为什么“打了转向灯也不能马上转弯或变道”，以及行人过街时为什么除了看灯还要留意车辆是否真正停稳让行。

## 小汽车钻进山里了吗？

穿过长长的隧道，到山的另一边

### 第 1 页

去汽车养护中心的路，一直朝青山脚下延伸。安安抱着小熊望向高高的大山：“妈妈，山这么高，小汽车要爬到山顶上去吗？”

**English**

The road to the car care center stretched toward the foot of a green mountain. An'an held Bear and looked up at the high slope. “Mom, the mountain is so tall—will our car have to climb over the top?”

### 第 2 页

妈妈指了指山脚：“不用爬山顶。你看前面，山脚下留好了一条穿过去的路。”一道整洁的半圆形入口出现在眼前。

**English**

Mom pointed toward the base of the mountain. “We don't need to climb the peak. Look ahead—there's a road built right through the foot of the mountain.” A neat arched portal came into view.

### 第 3 页

进入隧道前，妈妈先平稳放慢车速，又开启了小汽车的前照灯。仪表盘上亮起一盏绿色的小灯图案，车头前方照出温暖的光。

**English**

Before entering the tunnel, Mom smoothly slowed the car and switched on the headlights. A small green headlight icon lit up on the dashboard, and warm beams shone ahead.

### 第 4 页

小汽车顺着车道驶进隧道口。窗外明亮的阳光一下子留在了身后，安安轻轻说：“哇，我们真的钻进大山肚子里啦！”

**English**

Their little car followed its lane into the tunnel entrance. The bright outdoor sunshine stayed behind them, and An'an whispered, “Wow, we really drove inside the mountain!”

### 第 5 页

隧道顶上和两侧墙壁上，一排排照明灯整整齐齐地亮着。虽然没有太阳，前面的路面和车流依然看得清清楚楚。

**English**

Along the tunnel ceiling and side walls, neat rows of lights shone steadily. Even without sunshine, the road surface and traffic ahead remained clear to see.

### 第 6 页

安安问：“为什么刚进隧道时，这里的灯排得特别密呀？”妈妈说：“从太阳底下进来，眼睛要适应光线，亮一点能帮大家看清路。”

**English**

“Why are the lights spaced so closely near the entrance?” An'an asked. “When we come in from bright sunlight, our eyes need a moment to adjust,” Mom replied. “Extra light helps drivers see clearly.”

### 第 7 页

## 轮胎上的花纹，藏着什么秘密？

汽车养护中心里的脚印发现

### 第 1 页

**English**

After their light-blue car parked safely at the car care center, Mom checked around and helped An'an out. Technician Zhou, wearing a slate-gray work jacket with a blue collar, welcomed them with a smile. “Welcome in for your regular checkup!”

### 第 2 页

小汽车交由工作人员开进检查工位，妈妈牵着安安走进玻璃隔开的客户展示区。展架上摆着一个圆圆的黑色轮胎样品，安安一下子被吸引住了。

**English**

While staff drove the car into the inspection bay, Mom led An'an into the glass-separated visitor display lounge. On a display stand sat a round black sample tire that caught An'an's eye right away.

### 第 3 页

安安蹲下来看轮胎样品的表面。上面有一条条深深的直沟，还有许多弯弯的小槽。她好奇地问：“做得光溜溜的，不是更漂亮吗？”

**English**

An'an crouched down to look at the surface of the sample tire. Deep channels and curved little grooves ran across it. “Wouldn't it look nicer if it were completely smooth?” she asked curiously.

### 第 4 页

周叔叔走过来，指着旁边的科普小实验桌：“这些花纹和沟槽可不是为了好看，它们在路上要做很重要的工作呢。”

**English**

Technician Zhou walked over and gestured toward a small science demonstration table nearby. “Those patterns and grooves aren't just for looks—they have an important job to do on the road.”

### 第 5 页

周叔叔拿起一块底部光滑的橡胶块，轻轻按在有薄薄一层水的透明盘子里，再往前推——水被堵在下面，光滑橡胶块一下子滑开了。

### 第 6 页

**English**

Next, Zhou pressed down a rubber block carved with grooves. The water beneath escaped outward through the little channels, and the block gripped the bottom of the tray steadily!

“原来沟槽像小小的排水渠！”安安恍然大悟。周叔叔点头：“雨天路面有水时，许多轮胎的沟槽能帮助把接触处的水排开，帮助轮胎接触路面。”

**English**

**English**

An'an looked at a complete wheel display nearby and pointed to its shiny silver center. “Is that flower-shaped metal part in the middle part of the tire too?” she asked.

### 第 9 页

周叔叔解释说：“它们不是同一样东西哦。外面一圈有弹性的黑色橡胶叫轮胎，中间坚固的金属部分叫轮毂，合在一起才组成车轮。”

### 第 10 页

**English**

### 第 11 页

周叔叔指着沟槽底下的小凸起说：“汽车跑的路多了，轮胎会慢慢磨损，沟槽变浅后排水和抓地本领都会下降，所以需要大人定期检查。”

**English**

### 第 14 页

### 亲子共读

共读目标：了解洗车先冲浮尘、再打泡沫、轻柔清洗、冲净擦干的基本顺序，知道洗车废水需要专门收集与处理，并在生活中养成爱护物品的习惯。

家长提醒：真实洗车房内有水雾、湿滑地面与运转设备，儿童应在玻璃等候区或安全区域由成人陪同观看，不进入作业工位玩水；家中擦拭玩具车只使用清水和拧干的软布，不接触汽车清洁剂。

可以聊一聊：

- 为什么给小汽车洗澡时，要先用清水把浮尘和沙粒冲掉？
- 洗车流下来的泡沫和脏水去了哪里？为什么不能直接流进小河？
- 回家后，你想怎样像照顾小汽车一样整理和擦拭自己的玩具？

桌面小游戏：准备一块拧干的干净湿软布和一块干软布，在桌面陪孩子给玩具小车做一次“温柔洗澡”：先拂去灰尘，再擦拭车身、车窗和车轮，最后擦干并停回积木车库。只用少量清水，不设倒计时。

4–5 岁：按“冲水、泡泡、冲干净、擦干”四个步骤讲一讲小汽车洗澡的故事，再动手擦亮自己的玩具车。

6–8 岁：讨论为什么先冲沙粒能保护车漆，以及洗车房收集处理废水对保护河流和环境的意义。

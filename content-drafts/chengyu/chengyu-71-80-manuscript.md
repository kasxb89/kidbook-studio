## 半途而废 织到一半的布

成语见《礼记·中庸》；本书借鉴《后汉书·列女传》乐羊子妻劝学故事，作儿童化改编。

改编说明：保留求学归来与织布比喻，将剪断织物改为提问，并补充算数课堂、休息、分步学习、同伴求助与返家团聚情节；这些细节属于改编。

成语含义：比喻事情还没有做完，就中途停止，不再继续。

To stop halfway through a task and abandon it before it is finished.

寓意：遇到困难可以休息、求助和调整方法，再继续完成自己认定的事情。

When something is difficult, we can rest, ask for help, and try another way before carrying on toward our chosen goal.

日常用法：拼图还差一点，我们歇一会儿再拼，别半途而废。

We have only a little of the puzzle left. Let's rest and then finish, rather than give up halfway.

### 第 1 页

傍晚，乐羊子坐在窗边，看妻子把一根根线织进布里。

At dusk, Yue Yangzi sat by the window, watching his wife weave threads into cloth.

### 第 2 页

“我想跟老师学读书、学算数。”他望着远处的学堂说。

“I want to learn to read and work with numbers,” he said, looking toward the school.

### 第 3 页

妻子给他收好行囊：“遇到不懂的，就慢慢问、慢慢学。”

His wife packed his bundle. “When you don't understand, take your time and ask.”

### 第 4 页

第二天清晨，乐羊子背起行囊，沿着小路去求学。

The next morning, Yue Yangzi carried his bundle down the path to study.

### 第 5 页

学堂里，老师领他读竹简，还用竹筹教他算数。

At school, the teacher helped him read bamboo slips and count with wooden rods.

### 第 6 页

有的题要想好久。乐羊子算了又算，还是弄不明白。

Some problems took a long time. Yue Yangzi tried again and again but still felt puzzled.

### 第 7 页

日子一天天过去，他越来越想家，没学完的内容却还有许多。

As the days passed, he missed home more and more, with much still left to learn.

### 第 8 页

一天，他收起竹简，回到了家。妻子高兴地迎了出来。

One day, he packed his bamboo slips and returned home. His wife came out happily to welcome him.

### 第 9 页

吃过饭，妻子问：“最近学到了什么？还有哪里让你犯难？”

After their meal, his wife asked, “What have you learned? What is still difficult?”

### 第 10 页

乐羊子低声说：“还有好多没学会。我怕自己怎么学也学不好。”

“There is so much I don't know,” Yue Yangzi said softly. “I'm afraid I won't be able to learn it.”

### 第 11 页

妻子带他来到织机旁。米白的布已经织出一大段，后面还连着细线。

His wife led him to the loom. A long piece of cream cloth was woven, with fine threads still stretching beyond it.

### 第 12 页

“最开始，这里也只有几根线。”她轻轻摸着已经织好的地方。

“At first, there were only a few threads here,” she said, touching the woven cloth.

### 第 13 页

“有时织错了，我就退回一点，重新织。慢慢地，布才长起来。”

“Sometimes I make a mistake and go back a little. I weave it again, and the cloth slowly grows.”

### 第 14 页

她指着没织完的地方：“要是在这里停下，就做不成原来想要的那匹布了。”

She pointed to the unfinished section. “If I stop here, I won't have the whole cloth I wanted.”

### 第 15 页

乐羊子望着细线想：读书也要一点点接着学，才能把不懂的连起来。

Looking at the threads, Yue Yangzi thought: learning, too, happens a little at a time, joining what we know.

### 第 16 页

“我会想你，也会累。”他说。妻子点头：“歇一歇，再想想怎样继续。”

“I'll miss you, and I'll get tired,” he said. His wife nodded. “Rest, then think about how to carry on.”

### 第 17 页

休息过后，他把难题分成小步，又收好行囊，回学堂向老师请教。

After resting, he divided the difficult work into smaller steps, packed his bundle, and returned to ask his teacher.

### 第 18 页

这回，他先学懂一小段，再往下学。卡住了，就问老师，也和同伴一起试。

This time, he learned one small part before moving on. When he got stuck, he asked his teacher and tried with a classmate.

### 第 19 页

一段时间后，乐羊子完成了这次要学的内容，带着整理好的竹简回家。

After some time, Yue Yangzi finished the lessons he had set out to learn and brought his organized bamboo slips home.

### 第 20 页

妻子展开织好的布，乐羊子讲起学会的办法。屋里，线和学问都连成了新的模样。

His wife unfolded her finished cloth, and Yue Yangzi shared what he had learned. Both their efforts had become something new.

## 一诺千金 答应好的木偶戏

依据成语含义创作的原创童话，并非历史典故。

成语含义：形容说话算数，承诺很有分量，值得信任。

A promise worth a thousand pieces of gold: being trustworthy and keeping one's word.

寓意：答应之前认真考虑，答应之后努力做到；遇到困难，及时沟通并寻找办法。

Think before making a promise, work to keep it, and speak up promptly when difficulties arise.

日常用法：他说星期六来帮忙，果然来了，真是一诺千金。

He said he would help on Saturday, and he did. He really keeps his word.

### 第 1 页

小刺猬的生日快到了。他捧着一张自己画的邀请卡，来找小狐狸。

Hedgehog's birthday was coming. He brought Fox an invitation card he had painted himself.

### 第 2 页

“星期六下午，我给你演木偶戏！”小狐狸想了想，认真答应了。

“I'll put on a puppet show for you on Saturday afternoon!” Fox thought it over and made his promise.

### 第 3 页

他找出一只青蛙手偶、一只小鸭手偶，把小舞台擦得干干净净。

He found a frog puppet and a duck puppet and wiped his little stage clean.

### 第 4 页

青蛙跳过小河，小鸭摇摇摆摆。小狐狸一边排练，一边笑。

The frog hopped over a stream, and the duck waddled along. Fox laughed as he rehearsed.

### 第 5 页

星期四，小兔跑来邀请他：“周末集市开啦，星期六一起去吧！”

On Thursday, Rabbit invited him. “The weekend market is opening! Let's go on Saturday!”

### 第 6 页

小狐狸听得耳朵竖起来：甜甜的点心、会转的小风车，他都想看。

Fox's ears perked up. He wanted to see the sweet treats and the spinning pinwheels.

### 第 7 页

“下午最热闹！”小兔说。小狐狸看看邀请卡，心里忽然打了个结。

“The afternoon is the busiest!” said Rabbit. Fox looked at the invitation and suddenly felt torn.

### 第 8 页

他差点想说自己忘了，又把话咽了回去。小刺猬一定在等这场戏。

He almost said he had forgotten, then stopped himself. Hedgehog would be waiting for the show.

### 第 9 页

邀请卡上，小刺猬画了三个笑脸。小狐狸轻轻摸了摸那个属于自己的。

Hedgehog had painted three smiling faces on the card. Fox gently touched the one that looked like him.

### 第 10 页

他去找小兔：“我已经答应小刺猬了，星期六下午要留给木偶戏。”

He went to Rabbit. “I already promised Hedgehog. Saturday afternoon is for the puppet show.”

### 第 11 页

小兔点点头：“集市星期天也开。那我们星期天一起去！”

Rabbit nodded. “The market is open on Sunday too. We can go then!”

### 第 12 页

小狐狸高高兴兴回家。可一碰舞台，右边的木支架竟晃了起来。

Fox returned home happily. But when he touched the stage, its right wooden support wobbled.

### 第 13 页

他扶住支架，心里发慌：“要是演到一半倒了，可怎么办？”

He steadied the support and worried. “What if it falls during the show?”

### 第 14 页

这次，他没有悄悄躲开，而是告诉小刺猬：“舞台坏了，我正在想办法。”

This time he didn't hide. He told Hedgehog, “The stage needs fixing. I'm working on a way to do it.”

### 第 15 页

“我们一起看看吧。”小刺猬说。小兔也请来了会修木头的熊伯伯。

“Let's look together,” said Hedgehog. Rabbit brought Bear, who knew how to repair wooden things.

### 第 16 页

熊伯伯找到松动的地方，装上一块支撑木。小伙伴们帮忙扶稳舞台。

Bear found the loose joint and fitted a wooden brace. The friends helped keep the stage steady.

### 第 17 页

小狐狸轻轻试了试：不晃了！青蛙和小鸭又能在蓝幕布前见面了。

Fox tested it gently. No wobble! The frog and duck could meet in front of the blue curtain again.

### 第 18 页

星期五晚上，他再排练一遍，把需要的东西一件件收进小箱子。

On Friday evening, he rehearsed once more and packed everything into a little box.

### 第 19 页

生日那天下午，小刺猬摆好点心，小兔在窗边挂起纸花。

On the birthday afternoon, Hedgehog set out snacks while Rabbit hung paper flowers by the window.

### 第 20 页

蓝幕布拉开，青蛙跳出来，小鸭跟着摇摇摆摆。木偶戏准时开始了。

The blue curtain opened. Out hopped the frog, followed by the waddling duck. The show began on time.

### 第 21 页

小刺猬笑得直拍手：“这只小鸭太好玩啦！”小狐狸也开心地笑了。

Hedgehog clapped and laughed. “That duck is so funny!” Fox beamed too.

### 第 22 页

第二天逛集市时，小狐狸心里暖暖的。守住一个承诺，也守住了朋友的信任。

At the market the next day, Fox felt warm inside. By keeping his promise, he had kept his friend's trust.

## 雪中送炭 雪天送来的热汤

依据成语含义创作的原创童话，并非历史典故。

成语含义：比喻在别人急需帮助时，给予及时而切实的支持。

Sending charcoal in snowy weather: offering timely, practical support when someone needs it most.

寓意：在别人困难的时候，先听听他需要什么，再送上合适的帮助。

When someone is having a hard time, first listen to what they need, then offer help that fits.

日常用法：我正发愁怎么搬这些箱子，你就来帮忙了，真是雪中送炭！

I was worrying about moving these boxes, and you came to help just when I needed it!

### 第 1 页

小獾和妈妈刚搬到森林边。新家的窗外，有一棵高高的松树。

Badger and his mother had just moved to the edge of the forest. A tall pine stood outside their new window.

### 第 2 页

他们把干粮放进储藏室，又把厚被子铺到床上，准备迎接冬天。

They put dry food in the pantry and spread a thick quilt on the bed, getting ready for winter.

### 第 3 页

没过几天，大雪来了。松枝低低垂着，小路也披上白白的一层。

A few days later, snow arrived. Pine branches bowed low, and a white layer covered the path.

### 第 4 页

夜里，储藏室的屋顶漏了水。滴答，滴答，水落在粮袋旁边。

That night, water leaked through the pantry roof. Drip, drip—it fell beside the food bags.

### 第 5 页

獾妈妈连忙把东西搬开，可几袋干粮已经湿了，角落里的被子也潮了。

Badger's mother moved things out of the way, but some food bags were wet, and a spare quilt in the corner was damp.

### 第 6 页

小獾裹着衣服，望着空了许多的架子，心里有些发愁。

Badger wrapped his coat around himself and worried at the nearly empty shelves.

### 第 7 页

第二天，小兔做好一束纸花，想送去庆祝小獾的新家。

The next day, Rabbit made a bunch of paper flowers to celebrate Badger's new home.

### 第 8 页

来到门口，她看见接水的盆和湿粮袋，连忙问：“你们还好吗？”

At the door, she saw a basin catching drips and wet food bags. “Are you all right?” she asked.

### 第 9 页

小獾说：“我们有点冷，今天能吃的东西也不多了。”

“We're a little cold,” said Badger. “And we don't have much food for today.”

### 第 10 页

小兔把纸花收好：“我先回去问妈妈，看看能带些什么来。”

Rabbit tucked the paper flowers away. “I'll ask my mother what we can bring.”

### 第 11 页

兔妈妈听完，问清小獾家的需要，找出一条干燥的厚毯子。

Rabbit's mother listened, checked what Badger's family needed, and found a thick, dry blanket.

### 第 12 页

她煮了一锅热汤。小兔把能直接吃的面包和果干装进篮子。

She cooked a pot of hot soup. Rabbit packed bread and dried fruit into a basket.

### 第 13 页

热汤盖好，毯子包好。妈妈提着汤锅，小兔抱起装干粮的篮子。

The soup was covered and the blanket wrapped. Mother carried the pot, and Rabbit picked up the food basket.

### 第 14 页

她们沿着清扫过的小路，一步一步走到小獾家。

They followed the cleared path, step by step, to Badger's home.

### 第 15 页

獾妈妈接过毯子，给小獾裹在肩上。软软的暖意慢慢围住了他。

Badger's mother took the blanket and wrapped it around him. Soft warmth settled over his shoulders.

### 第 16 页

四只小碗摆上桌，热汤冒着轻轻的白气。大家坐下来，慢慢喝。

Four little bowls went on the table, with wisps of steam rising from the soup. Everyone sat and sipped slowly.

### 第 17 页

“你们送来的，正是我们最需要的。”獾妈妈握着兔妈妈的手说。

“You brought just what we needed,” said Badger's mother, taking Rabbit's mother's hand.

### 第 18 页

下午，熊伯伯来帮忙，从屋里补好漏水的地方。储藏室终于不再滴答了。

That afternoon, Bear helped seal the leak from inside. At last, the pantry stopped dripping.

### 第 19 页

天放晴后，小兔再来，把纸花挂在窗边。这回，小獾开心地帮她挑位置。

When the sky cleared, Rabbit returned with the paper flowers. This time Badger happily helped choose a spot.

### 第 20 页

雪还堆在屋外，屋里却暖暖的。小獾记住了那天及时送来的热汤。

Snow still lay outside, but the room felt warm. Badger remembered the soup that had arrived just when he needed it.

## 取长补短 三位朋友的游园会海报

依据成语含义创作的原创童话，并非历史典故。

成语含义：吸取别人的长处，弥补自己的不足。

To learn from another person's strengths and improve one's own weaker skills.

寓意：发现彼此的长处，愿意学习和分享，就能一起进步。

Notice one another's strengths, share what you know, and learn from each other.

日常用法：你会画画，我会写字，我们可以取长补短。

You draw well and I write neatly. We can learn from each other's strengths.

### 第 1 页

森林要办游园会，三位朋友都想画一张海报，邀请大家来玩。

The forest was holding a fair. Three friends each wanted to make a poster inviting everyone.

### 第 2 页

小兔抱来颜料，小乌龟摆好毛笔，小松鼠摊开一张大纸。

Rabbit brought paints, Tortoise set out brushes, and Squirrel spread a large sheet of paper.

### 第 3 页

小兔画的小动物活灵活现，可写上时间和地点后，纸上挤得快没空了。

Rabbit's animals looked lively, but once she added the time and place, her poster was almost too crowded.

### 第 4 页

小乌龟的字一行行排得整齐，却不知道用什么颜色，海报显得有些单调。

Tortoise's writing was neat, but he wasn't sure which colors to choose. His poster looked a little plain.

### 第 5 页

小松鼠留好了每块位置，可画出的小兔像个圆球，自己也认不出来。

Squirrel planned every space, but his rabbit drawing looked like a round ball, even to him.

### 第 6 页

三张海报摆在一起。小兔叹气，小乌龟皱眉，小松鼠挠了挠头。

They set out all three posters. Rabbit sighed, Tortoise frowned, and Squirrel scratched his head.

### 第 7 页

小乌龟先说：“小兔，你画的耳朵和眼睛，一看就知道是谁！”

“Rabbit, your ears and eyes make it easy to tell who's who!” Tortoise said first.

### 第 8 页

小兔也发现：“小松鼠，你给每一部分留的位置，真舒服呀。”

Rabbit noticed too. “Squirrel, you've left just the right amount of space for each part.”

### 第 9 页

小松鼠凑近小乌龟的纸：“你的字这么整齐，可以教教我们吗？”

Squirrel leaned toward Tortoise's paper. “Your writing is so neat. Will you teach us?”

### 第 10 页

小乌龟笑了：“可以！我也想学学，怎样把颜色搭得好看。”

Tortoise smiled. “Yes! I'd like to learn how to choose colors that work together.”

### 第 11 页

小兔画了两只小耳朵，让小松鼠慢慢试。小松鼠这回画得像多了。

Rabbit drew two little ears for Squirrel to try. This time his drawing looked much more like a rabbit.

### 第 12 页

“不如一起做一张大海报！”三个脑袋凑到同一张空白纸前。

“Let's make one big poster together!” Three heads leaned over a fresh sheet.

### 第 13 页

小松鼠先安排位置：上面留给名字，中间画游园路线，下面放小提示。

Squirrel planned the spaces: a title at the top, a fair map in the middle, and little reminders below.

### 第 14 页

小兔在中间画出花园、游戏摊和笑眯眯的小伙伴。

Rabbit drew a garden, game stalls, and smiling friends in the middle.

### 第 15 页

小乌龟慢慢写好内容，提醒大家：每一行都要给下一行留些地方。

Tortoise added the information carefully. “Each line needs room for the next,” he reminded them.

### 第 16 页

三个人退后看看，又把一朵挡住路线的小花挪到了旁边。

They stepped back to look, then moved a flower that was covering part of the route.

### 第 17 页

海报挂上木板，经过的朋友停下来，很快就找到自己想玩的地方。

They hung the poster on the board. Passing friends stopped and quickly found the activities they liked.

### 第 18 页

“原来，我们会的东西能互相帮忙。”小松鼠望着海报，笑了起来。

“The things we know can help each other,” Squirrel said with a smile.

### 第 19 页

回到桌前，他们各做一张小邀请卡，再试试刚学会的新办法。

Back at the table, each made a little invitation card, trying the new skills they had learned.

### 第 20 页

小兔留出了空白，小乌龟添了颜色，小松鼠画好了耳朵。三张小卡，都比刚才更好了。

Rabbit left room, Tortoise added color, and Squirrel drew clear ears. Each little card was better than before.

## 将心比心 小熊也想把话说完

依据成语含义创作的原创童话，并非历史典故。

成语含义：拿自己的感受去体会别人的处境，设身处地为别人着想。

To put oneself in another person's place and consider their feelings.

寓意：试着站在别人的位置体会他的感受，也认真听他说出自己的需要。

Imagine how someone else feels in their situation, and listen to what they need.

日常用法：你也希望别人认真听你说话，我们将心比心，先听他说完。

You want others to listen when you speak. Let's put ourselves in his place and let him finish.

### 第 1 页

森林露营日快到了，小熊、小松鼠和小兔坐到树下，一起商量带些什么。

The forest camping day was coming. Bear, Squirrel, and Rabbit sat beneath a tree to plan what to bring.

### 第 2 页

小熊有好多主意：带灯笼、讲故事，还要准备一张软软的垫子。

Bear had so many ideas: a lantern, stories, and a soft mat to sit on.

寓意：先了解更多信息，听听不同的人知道什么，再判断事情的全貌。

Gather more information and listen to different perspectives before deciding what the whole is like.

日常用法：只看这一张照片还不了解事情全貌，容易盲人摸象。

One photograph doesn't show the whole situation. We could mistake a small part for the whole.

### 第 1 页

村里来了一头大象。它站在宽宽的院子里，耳朵轻轻扇动。

An elephant came to the village. It stood in a wide yard, gently fanning its ears.

### 第 2 页

阿青、阿黄、阿蓝和阿紫很想认识它。他们看不见，常靠听、摸和询问了解新事物。

A-Qing, A-Huang, A-Lan, and A-Zi wanted to know it. They were blind and often learned by listening, touching, and asking questions.

### 第 3 页

照料员请他们慢慢来，陪着每个人，去摸大象不同的部位。

The caretaker invited them to take their time and helped each explore a different part of the elephant.

### 第 4 页

阿青摸到一条粗粗的腿：“原来，大象像一根结实的柱子！”

A-Qing felt a thick leg. “So an elephant is like a sturdy pillar!”

### 第 5 页

阿黄摸到宽宽的耳朵：“不对呀，它像一把大扇子！”

A-Huang felt a broad ear. “No, it's like a large fan!”

### 第 6 页

阿紫摸到细细的尾巴：“我摸到的，明明像一根绳子。”

A-Zi felt the thin tail. “What I felt was surely like a rope.”

### 第 7 页

阿蓝摸到弯弯的长鼻子：“它像一根会弯的长管子，还会动呢。”

A-Lan felt the curved trunk. “It's like a long bendy tube—and it moves.”

### 第 8 页

四个人坐下来，都想告诉朋友：自己已经知道整头大象是什么样了。

The four sat down, each eager to tell the others what the whole elephant was like.

### 第 9 页

“是柱子！”“是扇子！”“是绳子！”他们越说越急，谁也说服不了谁。

“A pillar!” “A fan!” “A rope!” They grew more impatient, but no one could convince the others.

### 第 10 页

照料员问：“你们摸到的，是大象同一个地方吗？”大家慢慢安静下来。

The caretaker asked, “Did you all touch the same part of the elephant?” Slowly, everyone grew quiet.

### 第 11 页

这回，阿青也摸摸耳朵，阿黄也摸摸腿。果然，每个地方都不一样。

This time, A-Qing felt the ear, and A-Huang felt a leg. Each part was different indeed.

### 第 12 页

他们又轮流了解别的部位，把各自摸到的，仔细讲给朋友听。

They took turns exploring more parts and carefully described what they had felt to one another.

### 第 13 页

粗粗的腿、宽宽的耳朵、长长的鼻子，还有细尾巴，原来都在同一头大象身上。

Thick legs, broad ears, a long trunk, and a thin tail—all belonged to the same elephant.

### 第 14 页

阿青说：“我只摸到腿，就以为那是全部。原来，我还知道得太少。”

A-Qing said, “I felt only the leg and thought it was the whole. I still had so much to learn.”

### 第 15 页

只知道一部分，就急着判断全部，这就是盲人摸象。多听、多问，才能了解更多。

Knowing only one part and rushing to judge the whole is called mistaking a part for the whole. Listening and asking help us learn more.

### 第 16 页

大象轻轻甩了甩鼻子。朋友们笑着再问一个问题，这一次，谁也不急着下结论。

The elephant gently swayed its trunk. The friends smiled and asked another question. This time, no one rushed to a conclusion.
